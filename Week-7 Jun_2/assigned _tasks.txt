T1 : Full Feature Preparation Pipeline - MEDIUM
Take your Week 4 ETL dataset (or any dataset with 8+ columns, mixed types, and some nulls). 
Apply the full prep pipeline: 
    (1) drop low-value columns with justification,
    (2) impute missing values using at least 2 strategies,
    (3) encode all categoricals appropriately,
    (4) scale numerics with StandardScaler, 
    (5) perform a 80/20 train-test split.
Output: a clean X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test and a written comment for every decision made.

T2 : Feature Engineering Challenge - HARD
Using a datetime-rich dataset (e.g., Uber rides, sales transactions, AirBnb listings),
    engineer at least 6 new features: extract day-of-week, 
                                      hour,
                                      is_weekend from timestamps;
    create 2 interaction features (multiply/ratio);
    apply log transform to at least 1 skewed numeric column; bin one continuous variable.
Then compare model accuracy (use any simple classifier) before and after your engineered features. 
Document the accuracy delta and explain why each feature helps.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T3 : Encoding Strategy Comparison - HARD
Take a dataset with at least 3 categorical columns of varying cardinality (low, medium, high). 
Apply:
        Label Encoding,
        One-Hot Encoding,
        Ordinal Encoding (where appropriate) to each. 
Train the same Logistic Regression model on each encoded version. 
Measure and compare accuracy, training time, and feature count for each strategy. 
Write a 1-paragraph recommendation on which encoding approach to use and why, backed by your results.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
T4 : Scaler Sensitivity Experiment - HARD
Take a dataset with mixed-scale numeric features. 
Train a KNN classifier (k=5) and an SVM on the raw (unscaled) data, then with StandardScaler, MinMaxScaler, and RobustScaler.
Record accuracy for all 8 combinations (2 models × 4 scaling options) in a results table. 
Inject 5 outlier rows artificially and re-run.
Which scaler is most robust?
Visualize the results as a grouped bar chart
write a conclusion explaining the practical takeaway for ML practitioners.
